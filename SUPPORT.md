# Get help with the guide

[← Home](README.md) · [Editorial policy](docs/editorial-policy.md)

- **A song does not sound right:** start with [common fixes](docs/troubleshooting.md) and [prompting instructions](docs/prompting.md).
- **A link, translation or product claim needs correcting:** [report a content problem](https://github.com/aimusicmaker/awesome-suno-creator-guide/issues/new?template=content-correction.yml).
- **You have a useful example:** [submit a creation case](https://github.com/aimusicmaker/awesome-suno-creator-guide/issues/new?template=creation-case.yml).
- **An account, payment or MusicMaker service issue:** contact [MusicMaker support](mailto:contact@musicmaker.im).
- **A Suno account or service issue:** use the [Suno Help Center](https://help.suno.com/).

For public reports, share the relevant prompt and settings, not passwords, payment receipts, private recordings or personal account details. For a private community-conduct concern, contact [MusicMaker](mailto:contact@musicmaker.im) and identify this repository.

The [MusicMaker organization](https://github.com/aimusicmaker) maintains this repository. We do not promise a fixed response time.
