# Community code of conduct

We want new songwriters, experienced musicians and video creators to be able to learn from one another.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work and the evidence with respect.
- Welcome beginners and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Credit creators and share only material you have permission to publish.
- Describe limitations and failures honestly.
- Keep feedback specific enough to act on.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, publishing private information, impersonation and repeated unsolicited promotion are not allowed. Do not attack a creator because they use or choose not to use AI tools.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to this repository's issues, pull requests and other spaces maintained for the guide. MusicMaker maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close off-topic discussions, warn contributors or restrict repeat offenders. Responses should reflect the seriousness and repetition of the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to [MusicMaker](mailto:contact@musicmaker.im), naming this repository and including relevant links. Do not post private evidence in an issue. Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people needed to assess and handle the report.

Participants may ask for a decision to be reviewed through the same contact channel.
