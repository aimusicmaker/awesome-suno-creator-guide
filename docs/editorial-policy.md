# Who makes this guide, and how we check it

[← Home](../README.md) · [Sources](sources.md) · [Get help](../SUPPORT.md)

## Publisher and maintainers

[MusicMaker](https://musicmaker.im/) publishes and maintains this guide through the [aimusicmaker GitHub organization](https://github.com/aimusicmaker). MusicMaker also operates the MusicMaker creation and detection tools recommended here.

The guide helps new songwriters and video creators choose a direction, understand the controls, and improve their next take. Suno product guidance is linked to Suno's own documentation. MusicMaker controls and account requirements are described separately.

Repository [contributions](https://github.com/aimusicmaker/awesome-suno-creator-guide/graphs/contributors) and [file history](https://github.com/aimusicmaker/awesome-suno-creator-guide/commits/main/) show who changed the material. These records establish responsibility for edits; we do not assign professional credentials to contributors.

## How material is selected and checked

| Material | What we check | What readers receive |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Official instructions | Original page, applicable model, access conditions and check date | A source link beside the claim |
| Community examples | Creator, original post, publication date, disclosed inputs and method | A preview, source link and concise explanation of what can be learned |
| MusicMaker catalog | Public track details, published Style and tags | Listening links and attributed source descriptions |
| Our exercises | Clear inputs, original lyrics, intended use and listening criteria | Copyable instructions with an explicit test status |
| Completed tests | Actual inputs, settings, model, date, output and observations | A record that separates what happened from our interpretation |

The [source notes](sources.md) name the retrieval method for each collection. An API record, a page inspection, a completed generation and an audio evaluation are different kinds of evidence.

AI assistance was used to draft and translate guide material, create original cover artwork and help review edits. Generation prompts are linked in the [artwork notes](../assets/README.md). Automated content checks verify navigation and file consistency; they do not establish music quality or detection accuracy.

## Corrections and updates

[Report an error or broken source](https://github.com/aimusicmaker/awesome-suno-creator-guide/issues/new?template=content-correction.yml). Include the page, affected claim and a supporting source. Maintainers review the evidence, update the relevant source note, regenerate the translated/mobile entry pages where needed, and run the repository checks.

Dates beside product claims identify the last source check. Change those dates only after checking the relevant material again. Historical examples retain the model and publication date associated with their original work.

## Share useful experience

[Submit a creation case](https://github.com/aimusicmaker/awesome-suno-creator-guide/issues/new?template=creation-case.yml) with your exact inputs and an output you can share. A failed take with one useful correction can teach more than an unexplained success.

Use the [listening log](../templates/listening-log.md) to compare two takes. The [practice test plan](practice-test-plan.md) defines three focused exercises and a separate detector check. Planned tests become published results only after the work is completed and recorded.

The [affiliate program](https://musicmaker.im/affiliate-program/) is an invitation to partner with MusicMaker. Community examples and Suno references remain credited to their creators. Media rights are explained in the [artwork notes](../assets/README.md).
