## Choose your starting point

| Your material | Start here | Official basis |
|:--|:--|:--|
| A sentence describing an idea | v6 Simple: describe the result and supply permitted references | [v6 FAQ](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481) |
| Your own lyrics plus a style brief | Custom: keep the lyrics and musical instructions separate | [Using your own lyrics](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2415873) |
| A good song with one weak passage | Select the passage and try Replace Section | [Song Editor](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/6141505) |
| A song that needs a continuation | Use Extend; listen across the join afterward | [v6 features](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924801) |

The current family is **v6, v6-wild, and v6-mini**. The first two require Pro/Premier; mini is available on all plans. Older songs remain in your library, but new iterations use the v6 family. These are current product facts from the [v6 FAQ](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481), not assumptions based on historical community posts.

## Four editing briefs

> “Preview alternate versions in the Edits Library on the right.”
> — [Suno, How to Use: Song Editor](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/6141505), May 30, 2025

The useful habit is to compare alternatives before committing. Save your original take first. These are requested changes, not promises that the model will preserve every other detail.

### 1. Give the chorus room / 让副歌更开阔

Select the chorus and choose **Replace Section**. Try this instruction in the edit prompt:

```text
Make this chorus feel wider: add warm bass and light backing harmonies.
Keep the lead vocal clear and the lyrics unchanged.
Avoid adding a busy lead instrument.
```

Check the transition into the chorus and whether the words stayed intact. If the whole song changes, return to the saved original and narrow your selected region.

### 2. Fix a rushed line / 改掉唱得太赶的一句

Select the relevant region and choose **Edit Lyrics**. Replace a long line with shorter words, then generate the replacement section. Paste lyrics, not the explanation, into that field.

Original line from our recipe:

```text
The city folds its windows closed
```

Shorter alternative:

```text
The windows close
```

Listen for pronunciation and the joins on both sides; a shorter line can change the phrasing.

### 3. Finish gently / 补一段自然收尾

The Song Editor guide describes the **+** at the right end for an extension, or **Quick Extend** for an automatic continuation. For a prompted extension:

```text
Continue into a quiet outro. Let the drums drop away,
then resolve with guitar and one sustained piano chord.
No new verse and no additional lyrics.
```

Listen across the join. If the song is already complete and merely ends too sharply, try a fade instead of adding another section.

### 4. Make a clean background passage / 为旁白腾出空间

Select the intended passage and use **Replace Section**:

```text
Make this passage a sparse instrumental bed for spoken narration.
Soft piano, restrained bass, no lead vocal, no busy melody.
Keep the energy steady and avoid a sudden drum fill.
```

Check for unwanted vocals. For a precise clip length, use **Crop** and appropriate fades in the editor, or trim the export in your video editor. A written duration request alone is not precise timing control.

## Before you remix someone else's work

Suno's [Remix guide](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/6050497) says the creator must enable remixes, and that remix rights remain with the original creator; a song that did not start as your own cannot be monetized under that guidance. A historical contest post is not current permission. [Read the release checklist](detector-and-release.md) before distribution.

Writing your own lyrics preserves your rights in that input, according to the [lyrics guide](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2415873). This is separate from rights in the generated recording and from copyright protection.
