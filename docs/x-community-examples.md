# X community examples

[English homepage](../README.md) · [中文首页](../README_ZH.md)

Nine creator-owned X posts, selected for a finished work, an explicit workflow or a useful music prompt. This is a mixed selection, not a popularity ranking: established tutorials sit alongside smaller community experiments. “Nonofficial” describes the publishing account; it does not certify the absence of sponsorship or platform relationships.

Click each preview to open the original post. Images remain hosted by their sources; author rights remain with their owners. An X login may be required. Historical examples retain their publication dates and do not establish current model availability.

**Evidence checked September 23, 2026:** post text, author identity, media metadata and engagement through the public FxTwitter API. This is not native X interface verification, an audio-quality assessment or a generation reproduction. Counts below are dated API snapshots. Our practice suggestions are original, untested exercises; they are not the authors’ hidden prompts.

## Tech house from a short prompt

[![Tech house from a short prompt — source preview by @maxescu](https://pbs.twimg.com/amplify_video_thumb/2049444325596286976/img/lqCxWmk86QgVYb-a.jpg)](https://x.com/maxescu/status/2049444543360336167)

**[@maxescu](https://x.com/maxescu/status/2049444543360336167) · 2026-04-29** — 109 likes / 11,147 views on X at the check above. [Verification record](https://api.fxtwitter.com/maxescu/status/2049444543360336167).

The author describes training a Suno v5.5 Custom Model on 61 of his own productions, then making a bass-led dance track. The thread follows it through choreography and editing.

**What is disclosed:** Published music prompt; custom-model input also matters.

**Published music prompt** ([author’s Suno step](https://x.com/maxescu/status/2049444564684222946)):

```text
Tech House, 124 BPM, Bass-Driven
```

**Try this — our exercise, not generation-tested:** Choose one dance genre, a tempo and a bass character. Compare two takes before adding more adjectives. A plain prompt does not recreate a custom model trained on someone else’s music.
