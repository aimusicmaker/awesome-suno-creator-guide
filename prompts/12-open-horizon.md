# Open Horizon

[← All recipes](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

**Sound:** Cinematic travel · **Use:** A landscape reveal

Original editorial recipe. Not generation-tested; no linked recording is claimed as its output.

**Try this recipe:** open [Suno](https://suno.com/create) or [MusicMaker](https://musicmaker.im/ai-song-generator/), then select **Custom**. Use **Styles** in Suno or **Music Style** in MusicMaker for the first block. [Field guide](../docs/prompting.md#choose-a-creation-tool).

## Paste into Style

```text
Instrumental cinematic travel cue, felt piano opening, warm sustained
strings and soft low percussion. Patient beginning, gradual lift into a
broad hopeful theme, then a gentle resolved ending. No choir, lead vocal
or trailer-style impacts.
```

## Instrumental setup

Turn **Instrumental on** and leave Lyrics empty. If your interface does not provide this control, keep the instrumental request in Style and check the result for unwanted voices.

## Make it yours

Replace strings with ambient guitar for a smaller, more personal travel diary.

## Listen for

Does the lift feel earned? Is there a complete ending you can cut to without chopping off a note?

## If it misses

If the build peaks too early, simplify the opening request; if timing still differs, edit the picture around the best musical transition.

Save the model, settings, and take in your [listening log](../templates/listening-log.md). Change one element per attempt.
