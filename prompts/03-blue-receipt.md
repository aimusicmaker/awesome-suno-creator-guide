# Blue Receipt

[← All recipes](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

**Sound:** R&B · **Use:** A restrained late-night confession

Original editorial recipe. Not generation-tested; no linked recording is claimed as its output.

**Try this recipe:** open [Suno](https://suno.com/create) or [MusicMaker](https://musicmaker.im/ai-song-generator/), then select **Custom**. Use **Styles** in Suno or **Music Style** in MusicMaker for the first block. [Field guide](../docs/prompting.md#choose-a-creation-tool).

## Paste into Style

```text
Slow contemporary R&B, sparse electric piano, warm sub bass and a laid-
back drum pocket. Close breathy alto lead, restrained melodic runs, dry
intelligible words. Verse leaves space between phrases; chorus adds soft
layered harmonies.
```

## Paste into Lyrics

Turn **Instrumental off**. These original lyrics are a compact sketch; use the [structure guide](../docs/lyrics-and-structure.md) to develop a longer song.

```text
[Verse]
Your message glows at half past two
I type a line, then start anew
The kettle clicks, the room goes still
I leave the cup beside the sill

[Chorus]
I read it, I know
I'm learning to let the quiet grow
No answer tonight
Just blue against the kitchen light

[Bridge]
Some things can wait until the day
I put the little screen away

[Chorus]
I read it, I know
I'm learning to let the quiet grow
```

## Make it yours

Change the lead description from breathy alto to clear, conversational tenor.

## Listen for

Listen for space after each phrase; the harmonies should support the hook rather than obscure it.

## If it misses

If runs swallow the words, replace “breathy” with “plain, syllabic delivery” and shorten the chorus.

Save the model, settings, and take in your [listening log](../templates/listening-log.md). Change one element per attempt.
