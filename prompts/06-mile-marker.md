# Mile Marker

[← All recipes](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

**Sound:** Country · **Use:** A road-trip story with a specific detail

Original editorial recipe. Not generation-tested; no linked recording is claimed as its output.

**Try this recipe:** open [Suno](https://suno.com/create) or [MusicMaker](https://musicmaker.im/ai-song-generator/), then select **Custom**. Use **Styles** in Suno or **Music Style** in MusicMaker for the first block. [Field guide](../docs/prompting.md#choose-a-creation-tool).

## Paste into Style

```text
Mid-tempo contemporary country, acoustic rhythm guitar, warm electric
bass, steady drums and restrained pedal steel fills. Natural
storytelling baritone vocal. Dry verses, fuller chorus; bittersweet and
grounded, no spoken introduction.
```

## Paste into Lyrics

Turn **Instrumental off**. These original lyrics are a compact sketch; use the [structure guide](../docs/lyrics-and-structure.md) to develop a longer song.

```text
[Verse]
At mile thirteen the diner closed
The sign still leans beside the road
I keep a receipt from ninety-nine
Your coffee and a slice of mine

[Chorus]
Every mile brings something back
A worn-out booth, a railroad track
I can't turn this car around
But I can take you to the next town

[Outro]
To the next town
```

## Make it yours

Swap pedal steel for fiddle, leaving the vocal and pace unchanged.

## Listen for

Does the story remain understandable without the lyric sheet? Do the fills leave the singer room?

## If it misses

If the voice rushes the year, write “ninety-nine” in full, as here, and simplify the line around it.
