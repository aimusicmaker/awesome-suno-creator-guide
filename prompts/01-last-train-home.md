# Last Train Home

[← All recipes](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

**Sound:** Indie pop · **Use:** A nostalgic first song

Original editorial recipe. Not generation-tested; no linked recording is claimed as its output.

## Paste into Style

```text
Warm indie pop, relaxed mid-tempo groove, muted electric guitar, rounded
bass and dry drums. Intimate low lead vocal in the verse; a brighter
chorus with light backing harmonies. Nostalgic but hopeful. Short intro,
clear words, restrained ending.
```

Turn **Instrumental off**. These original lyrics are a compact sketch; use the [structure guide](../docs/lyrics-and-structure.md) to develop a longer song.

[Chorus]

## Make it yours

Replace muted electric guitar with soft upright piano; keep the lyrics unchanged.

## Listen for

The chorus should feel more open than the verse, without burying the words.
