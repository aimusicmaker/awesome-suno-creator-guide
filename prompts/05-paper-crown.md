# Paper Crown

[← All recipes](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

**Sound:** Pop punk · **Use:** A defiant, playful singalong

Original editorial recipe. Not generation-tested; no linked recording is claimed as its output.

**Try this recipe:** open [Suno](https://suno.com/create) or [MusicMaker](https://musicmaker.im/ai-song-generator/), then select **Custom**. Use **Styles** in Suno or **Music Style** in MusicMaker for the first block. [Field guide](../docs/prompting.md#choose-a-creation-tool).

## Paste into Style

```text
Fast melodic pop punk, crunchy rhythm guitars, driving acoustic drums
and bright bass. Earnest slightly rough lead vocal, tight short phrases.
Palm-muted verse opens into a wide chorus; gang vocals only on the final
hook. Punchy clean ending.
```

## Paste into Lyrics

Turn **Instrumental off**. These original lyrics are a compact sketch; use the [structure guide](../docs/lyrics-and-structure.md) to develop a longer song.

```text
[Verse]
I wore your rules like borrowed shoes
Too tight to run, too old to choose
I left them by the garden gate
And took the bus a little late

[Chorus]
Keep your paper crown
I'm not coming down
Got my own two feet
And a whole new street

[Bridge]
I don't need a throne to stand
Just a ticket in my hand

[Chorus]
Keep your paper crown
I'm not coming down
```

## Make it yours

Change the rhythm guitars to bright clean guitars while keeping the drums energetic.

## Listen for

Is the hook intelligible when the band gets loud? Is the verse narrower than the chorus?

## If it misses

If the vocal becomes a scream, use “melodic sung lead, clear consonants” and reduce competing vocal descriptors.
