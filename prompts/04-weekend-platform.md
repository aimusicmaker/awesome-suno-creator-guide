**Sound:** Dance pop · **Use:** An upbeat chorus for a social clip

Original editorial recipe. Not generation-tested; no linked recording is claimed as its output.

**Try this recipe:** open [Suno](https://suno.com/create) or [MusicMaker](https://musicmaker.im/ai-song-generator/), then select **Custom**. Use **Styles** in Suno or **Music Style** in MusicMaker for the first block. [Field guide](../docs/prompting.md#choose-a-creation-tool).

## Paste into Style

```text
Bright dance pop, buoyant four-on-the-floor kick, elastic bass, clipped
guitar and sparkling synth accents. Clear energetic lead vocal. Short
verse, rising pre-chorus, immediate singable chorus with group responses
on its final line.
```

## Paste into Lyrics

Turn **Instrumental off**. These original lyrics are a compact sketch; use the [structure guide](../docs/lyrics-and-structure.md) to develop a longer song.

```text
[Verse]
Friday written on my shoes
Nothing in my bag to lose
Tickets folded in my hand
Meet me where the buses stand

[Pre-Chorus]
One more turn, we see the sea
Count the streetlights out with me

[Chorus]
We got all weekend
And nowhere we have to be
We got all weekend
Save the window seat for me

[Outro]
Save the window seat for me
```

## Make it yours

Keep the lyric but replace sparkling synths with piano chords for a more organic dance feel.

## Listen for

Does the chorus give you one phrase you can repeat after a single listen?

## If it misses

If the intro takes too long, choose a chorus excerpt in your editor; a short-intro prompt cannot enforce an exact start time.
