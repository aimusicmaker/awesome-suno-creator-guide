# Birthday Table

[← All recipes](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

**Sound:** Acoustic celebration · **Use:** A personal birthday gift

Original editorial recipe. Not generation-tested; no linked recording is claimed as its output.

## Paste into Style

```text
Warm acoustic pop celebration, lightly strummed ukulele, soft handclaps
and simple bass. Friendly clear lead vocal with a small group joining
the chorus. Relaxed singalong, affectionate and playful, gentle ending.
```

## Paste into Lyrics

Turn **Instrumental off**. These original lyrics are a compact sketch; use the [structure guide](../docs/lyrics-and-structure.md) to develop a longer song.

```text
[Verse]
The cake is leaning to the right
We saved your chair beside the light
Your stories make the dishes wait
We always eat a little late

[Chorus]
Here's to another trip around
To all the good we've yet to find
We saved the biggest wish for you
And left the candles up to you

[Outro]
We left the candles up to you
```

## Make it yours

Replace “Your stories make the dishes wait” with a similarly short line about the recipient’s real habit.

## Listen for

Can everyone sing the chorus comfortably? Is the personal detail recognizable?

## If it misses

If a name is mispronounced, test a phonetic spelling in a short draft before regenerating the whole song.

Save the model, settings, and take in your [listening log](../templates/listening-log.md). Change one element per attempt.
