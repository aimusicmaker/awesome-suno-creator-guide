# Window Seat

[← All recipes](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

**Sound:** Lo-fi study · **Use:** Background music that leaves attention free

Original editorial recipe. Not generation-tested; no linked recording is claimed as its output.

**Try this recipe:** open [Suno](https://suno.com/create) or [MusicMaker](https://musicmaker.im/ai-song-generator/), then select **Custom**. Use **Styles** in Suno or **Music Style** in MusicMaker for the first block. [Field guide](../docs/prompting.md#choose-a-creation-tool).

## Paste into Style

```text
Instrumental lo-fi hip-hop, slow relaxed groove, soft electric piano,
rounded bass and understated brushed drums. Repeating mellow motif,
light tape texture, stable low intensity, smooth transitions. No
singing, spoken words or vocal chops.
```

## Instrumental setup

Turn **Instrumental on** and leave Lyrics empty. If your interface does not provide this control, keep the instrumental request in Style and check the result for unwanted voices.

## Make it yours

Remove tape texture for a cleaner background. Keep the motif and instrumentation unchanged.

## Listen for

Play it quietly while reading: does any drum hit or melody change pull your attention away?

## If it misses

If voices appear, confirm instrumental mode is on and remove vocal-related language except the exclusion. Try a fresh generation.

Save the model, settings, and take in your [listening log](../templates/listening-log.md). Change one element per attempt.
