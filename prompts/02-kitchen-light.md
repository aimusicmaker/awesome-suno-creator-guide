# Kitchen Light

[← All recipes](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

**Sound:** Acoustic folk · **Use:** An intimate family memory

Original editorial recipe. Not generation-tested; no linked recording is claimed as its output.

**Try this recipe:** open [Suno](https://suno.com/create) or [MusicMaker](https://musicmaker.im/ai-song-generator/), then select **Custom**. Use **Styles** in Suno or **Music Style** in MusicMaker for the first block. [Field guide](../docs/prompting.md#choose-a-creation-tool).

## Paste into Style

```text
Intimate acoustic folk, unhurried fingerpicked guitar, soft upright
bass, close natural lead vocal. A small-room sound with light brushed
percussion entering in the chorus. Gentle, conversational phrasing; no
big orchestral climax.
```

## Paste into Lyrics

Turn **Instrumental off**. These original lyrics are a compact sketch; use the [structure guide](../docs/lyrics-and-structure.md) to develop a longer song.

```text
[Verse]
You left the flour on your sleeve
And told me what the apples need
A little salt, a little time
I wrote it down between the lines

[Chorus]
In the kitchen light
We can stay a little longer
Let the evening wait outside
In the kitchen light

[Outro]
A little salt, a little time
```

## Make it yours

Remove percussion entirely for a quieter voice-and-guitar arrangement.

## Listen for

Can the singer finish each line without rushing? Does the small-room feeling survive the chorus?

## If it misses

Shorten long phrases first. If the sound becomes too grand, remove extra instruments rather than adding more mood words.

Save the model, settings, and take in your [listening log](../templates/listening-log.md). Change one element per attempt.
